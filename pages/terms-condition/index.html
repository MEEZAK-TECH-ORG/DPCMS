Terms and Conditions

Destiny Promoters Cooperative

By registering on the Destiny Promoters Cooperative platform, every member acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to abide by the following Terms and Conditions:

  1. Potential Members are expected to pay a nonrefundable registration fee of ₦2,000 for Achievers thrift and ₦3,500 for Thrift4bussiness only.
  2. After making payment for your registration, you have 48hrs to pay a minimum of your one-week thrift amount into your virtual account for your contribution payment or else, your account will be deactivated and you will have to pay another registration money to activate your account.
  3. Note that, any mistake made during your registration is at your cost, Destiny Promoters Cooperative will not be held responsible for any of such mistakes.
  4. Members must credit their weekly Thrift of ₦1,500.00 (with addition of ₦100 for payment gateway changes) per single thrift into their virtual wallet account on or before 12:59am every Friday for Achievers Thrift and on or before 25th day of the second and third month for Thrift4bussiness thrift.
  5. If members fail to credit their virtual wallet account before 12:59am on a Particular Friday, it will attract a 100% default penalty fee for Achievers Thrift. While for Thrift4bussiness, if members fail to credit their virtual wallet account before 12:00noon of the 25th day on a Particular month, it will attract a default penalty fee of ₦8,000 per account.
  6. Every member must introduce at least one person with an active account under the Premium Thrift account who must be up to 6 months (25 weeks) on the system before the account can be qualified for ₦150,000.00 and foodstuff incentive. While for Thrift4bussiness, every member must introduce at least one person with an active account under the Thrift4bussiness account either in the current batch of opening the account or in the next batch before the account can be qualified for ₦150,000.00 and foodstuff incentive.
  7. Members doing Semestral Thrift plan must introduce five active downlines under the account within 40 days of its registration and the five (5) downlines to refer at least one active member each within the 25 weeks of contribution or it will no longer be termed Semestral Thrift and won't be able to withdraw after 25 weeks of contribution.
  8. Semestral Thrift account downlines must be nothing less than 6 months on the system while the 5 downlines must be Active and nothing less than 6months before the semestral account will be matured for a withdrawal.
  9. Uploading of fake proof of payments will lead to automatic cancellation of an account irrespective of its maturity stage.
  10. Every account must contribute the sum of ₦75,000 for Achievers Thrift, ₦100,000 for Thrift4bussiness before it can be matured for a withdrawal and other benefits.
  11. Termination of account attracts a termination fine of ₦10,000 per an account either for Achievers or Thrift4bussiness Thrift.
  12. Refunds relating to terminated accounts shall be processed within Five (5) weeks, subject to compliance with all applicable termination procedures and verification requirements.
  13. At collection of food items point, members must give consent for a picture shot.
  14. Food Products not collected on approved day of settlement will attract N4,000.00 penalty fine before another day of collection will be approved.
  15. Food Products not collected by members after 2 weeks of approved settlement day without any information from the involved member will automatically be used for Charity.
  16. Destiny Promoters Cooperative Empowerment Payment is within 7-10 working days from the date of withdrawal application upon meeting up with all expected requirements. However, DPCMS management reserves the right to adjust dates of payments in case of unforeseen circumstances. Notice will be given.
  17. Registered accounts are personal and cannot be transferred, assigned, or sold to another individual under any circumstances.
  18. An account can be suspended if the member goes against the cooperative policies and systems. Where a suspended account serves as a downline, the sponsoring upline shall lose that downline. If, as a result, the upline no longer meets the qualification requirements after six (6) months of registration, the upline may become ineligible for the ₦150,000 empowerment and food empowerment.
  19. A member is advised to provide a trusted next of kin, that will get his or her contribution refund in case the member dies.
  20. If a member dies before completing the fifty (50) week contribution cycle for Achievers Thrift or 6-Month Contribution cycle for Thrift4bussiness, Destiny Promoters Cooperative shall refund only the total amount contributed by the deceased to the registered next of kin. The empowerment benefits shall not be payable, as the account will no longer qualify for empowerment due to the absence of continuity.
  21. Destiny Promoters Cooperative contribution empowerment is after 50 weeks cycle for Premium Thrift and 25 weeks cycle for Semestral under Achievers Thrift and after 6-months for Thrift4business Thrift.
  22. Any payment made in the name of Destiny Promoters Cooperative that is not confirmed will require further investigation from the depositor which will require the depositor providing his or her statement of account and any other information that will be required by Destiny Promoters Cooperative to further investigate the transaction.
  23. Destiny Promoters Cooperative runs a mutual support system where members empower one another, what one member contributes is used to empower another. Funds are therefore in circulation among members and not held by the management but are already circulated among members within the empowerment system. The management is committed to managing the process transparently and diligently. The Cooperative also engage in viable business ventures for the benefit of all members. Please note that, the Cooperative is pioneered under the platform of Destiny Promoters Evangelical Outreach and collectively owned by all its members. We grow together, we own together, and we prosper together.
  24. All manually uploaded transaction will take within 24 to 48 hours before the transactions will be confirmed.
  25. Only Account with Active Downline(s) with no or maximum Defaults of 5 weeks will be eligible for ₦150,000 and foodstuffs incentive upon maturity of the account.
  26. You are expected to pay a clearance fee of ₦2,500 only per account both for Achievers and Thrift4business Thrift before you can be eligible for withdrawal.
  27. Every account holder in Destiny Promoters Cooperative must buy the company's branded T-shirt for Achievers Thrift before the person will get his/her complete empowerment. While for Thrift4bussiness, members are to pay an outreach support fund of ₦1,000. These are one-off payments per a profile.
  28. Here are multiple thrift accounts restriction policies:
    1. Members are to open a maximum of Thirty (30) accounts monthly for Achiever’s thrift and Fifty (50) accounts for thrift4business per batch.
    2. Accounts opened with different profiles but same bank account details for empowerment will lose 50% of their cash and food incentive empowerment benefits
    3. Bulk accounts stipulated at 100 only for achievers’ thrift can be paid weekly but with 60% empowerment return and ₦500,000 value of food incentives. Noncompliance to these policies will result to either termination of their thrift account or loss of 50% of your empowerment.
    4. For Bulk accounts stipulated at 31 to 100, members are to contact the admin office for bulk account opening and payment plan schedule.
    5. Members with interest to operate above 100 accounts are advised to contact the office for partnership plan options.
  29. No Active Downline within 25 weeks of registration for Achievers Thrift, payment at maturity will be ₦75,000.00 with no foodstuff incentives. While for Thrift4bussiness Thrift, No Active Downline within the batch of registration or the next batch, payment at maturity will be ₦100,000.00 with no foodstuff incentives.
  30. Participants without at least one active downline within six (6) months of registration for Achievers and within current or next batch for Thrift4bussiness, shall not qualify for food incentives. Food incentives are not promised entitlements but discretionary empowerment gifts provided by Destiny Prompters Cooperative to support the living of active members. Therefore, the Cooperative reserves the sole right to determine the food items, nature, quantity, timing, and conditions under which such incentives are distributed.
  31. Correction of mistakes, profile updating and referral update will be charged ₦2,000.00 per every action/correction.
  32. Active Members children of our Cooperative are eligible to attend our schools with 10% discount benefit on tuition.
  33. Active Members of our Cooperative are eligible to buy our Cooperative sales products with 3-5% discount benefit depending on volume of purchase.
  34. All members are expected to uphold the values, integrity, and reputation of the Cooperative at all times. Members must communicate respectfully and constructively in all forums. Any grievance or complaint must be channeled privately through the official internal complaint channels of DPCMS. Note that, social media platforms and general WhatsApp groups are NOT official complaint channels. Any member found dropping complaints, making negative, false, or harmful statements about the Cooperative outside the approved channels will be subject to disciplinary action. This may include immediate termination of the member’s account(s), irrespective of its maturity status. In the event of termination under this clause, a refund of the contributed capital amount, less any applicable charges, will be processed within 72 working hours.
  35. All members are expected to participate in at least one DPCMS Ministry/Cooperative Outreach Program within each calendar year. A maximum charge of ₦1,000.00 [One Thousand Naira Only] per account will be debited from member’s wallet as their outreach participation contribution. However, the exact amount to be debited will be determined based on the fundraising target and needs of the specific outreach program. This contribution supports our mandate to serve communities and advance the Kingdom of God.
