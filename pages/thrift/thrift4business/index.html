Thrift4Business

Thrift4Business is a structured six-month thrift and business empowerment programme operated by the empowerment arm of Destiny Promoters Evangelical Outreach (Destiny Promoters Cooperative (Onigbongbo) Multipurpose Society).

Designed for entrepreneurs and business owners, the programme helps members build disciplined savings while providing access to DPCMS-funded empowerment benefits subject to the programme requirements.

Thrift4Business runs in batches. Registration for each batch is open from the 1st through the last calendar day of the first month, while the required thrift payments are completed within the first three months. Eligible accounts mature at the end of the six-month cycle.

How It Works

A simple six-month thrift programme designed to help members contribute consistently and access DPCMS-funded empowerment benefits where applicable.

1

Registration

Register for an available Thrift4Business batch through the platform.

Registration is open from the 1st through the last calendar day of the first month of each batch.

A ₦3,500 registration fee applies to each thrift account and is included in the total amount payable for the programme.

For registration assistance, contact us via WhatsApp on 09020964738 or call 08184212487.

2

Thrift Payment

Each Thrift4Business account requires a total payment of ₦103,500 over the first three months of the thrift cycle.

Members make three monthly payments of ₦34,500, bringing the total amount paid to ₦103,500.

Of the total amount:

  • ₦100,000 represents the member's thrift contribution.
  • ₦3,500 represents the applicable registration fee.

At the completion of the required payment period, a member who has fulfilled the applicable programme requirements will have a thrift contribution balance of ₦100,000 toward settlement at maturity.

3

Payment Deadline & Late Payment

Members are expected to complete each required monthly thrift payment within the applicable payment period.

Payments made after the prescribed payment deadline may attract the applicable late payment penalty in accordance with the Thrift4Business Terms and Conditions.

Members are encouraged to make their contributions on time to keep their thrift accounts active and in good standing.

4

Referral & Empowerment Benefit

Members may qualify for an additional ₦50,000 empowerment benefit by successfully referring at least one eligible participant to Thrift4Business.

For a referral to qualify, the referred participant must:

  • Successfully register for Thrift4Business; and
  • Complete at least two months of the required thrift contributions.

Simply registering or creating an account does not qualify as a completed referral. The referred participant must satisfy the minimum two-month contribution requirement before the referral becomes eligible.

Upon completion of the six-month thrift cycle and fulfilment of all applicable programme requirements, a qualifying member receives the ₦100,000 thrift settlement plus the additional ₦50,000 DPCMS-funded empowerment benefit, bringing the total applicable settlement to ₦150,000.

Members who do not meet the referral requirement remain entitled to their applicable ₦100,000 thrift settlement at maturity, provided they have completed all required thrift contributions and satisfied the applicable programme conditions.

5

Maturity & Settlement

Each Thrift4Business cycle runs for six months.

Members complete their required thrift contributions during the first three months and, subject to fulfilment of the programme requirements, become eligible for settlement at the end of the six-month cycle.

A member who completes the required thrift payments but does not qualify for the referral empowerment benefit receives an applicable thrift settlement of ₦100,000.

A member who completes the required thrift payments and satisfies the qualifying referral requirement receives an applicable total empowerment of ₦150,000 with food incentives.

6

Account Termination

Thrift accounts that fail to meet the required contribution obligations may be terminated in accordance with the Thrift4Business Terms and Conditions.

Where a thrift account is terminated, any applicable refund, administrative charge or termination fee will be processed according to the programme's official Terms and Conditions.

Members are encouraged to review the applicable terms and conditions of the cooperative before registering and participating in a Thrift4Business batch.

About the Programme Operator

Thrift4Business is a product owned and operated by Destiny Promoters Cooperative (Onigbongbo) Multipurpose Society (DPCMS). The empowerment arm of Destiny Promoters Evangelical Outreach.

DPCMS is responsible for the administration of the Thrift4Business programme, including member registration, thrift contribution records, account management, applicable settlements, empowerment benefits and customer support.

Thrift4Business forms part of DPCMS's cooperative and business empowerment programmes for its members.
